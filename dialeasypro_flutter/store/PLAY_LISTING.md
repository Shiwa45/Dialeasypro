## Full description (max 4000)
DialSathi is the mobile app for sales and tele-calling teams that use the DialSathi CRM. Agents call leads from their own SIM, record every outcome and never miss a follow-up, while managers see the results live on the web dashboard.

You need a DialSathi workspace to sign in. Your company admin creates your login.

AUTO DIAL
• Start a call queue and DialSathi dials the next lead automatically through your phone's SIM.
• See the lead's name, status, notes and last call before you talk.
• Save the outcome in two taps: connected, busy, not reachable, interested, call back and more.

LEADS AND FOLLOW-UPS
• Your assigned leads in one list, with search and filters.
• Schedule follow-ups and get reminders on time, even when the app is closed.
• Add notes, update the status and see the full history of every lead.

CALL RECORDING (OPTIONAL)
• If your company turns it on, calls can be recorded through the phone's microphone after you agree to the in-app disclosure, so managers can review call quality. Recording follows your company's policy and local law.

WHATSAPP AND SMS
• Send approved WhatsApp templates and SMS to a lead in one tap.
• Read and reply to customers' WhatsApp messages in the inbox.

YOUR WORK AT A GLANCE
• Today's calls, talk time, connected calls and conversions.
• Call history with durations and outcomes.
• Attendance and breaks, when your company uses DialSathi HRMS.

BUILT FOR TEAMS IN INDIA
• Works with the phone and SIM you already have: no VoIP line or extra hardware.
• Each company's data is kept separate, and access is role-based.
