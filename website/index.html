How it works

One lead, start to finish, without switching tools.

Scroll through a single lead's journey in DialSathi.

1Capture

Leads arrive grouped and ready.

IndiaMART, Meta, Google Ads and your website send leads into one batch per source per day. Imports become named batches with duplicates removed.

2Distribute

Share a batch fairly in one click.

Round robin, equal split or least loaded, with an optional cap per agent. Leftovers stay unassigned and reported instead of overloading anyone.

3Call

Every call recorded and logged.

Click to call through Exotel, MCUBE or Knowlarity, or from the agent's phone. The disposition and next follow-up are set the moment they hang up.

4Follow up

WhatsApp does the chasing.

Approved templates go out one by one or in bulk through Interakt, AiSensy, Wati or Gupshup. Numbers on the DND registry are skipped automatically.

5Close & reward

The deal pays everyone correctly.

The won lead becomes a GST invoice, and the deal feeds the agent's incentive on this month's payslip.

Questions

Before you ask.

Do I pay for every agent separately?

No. Each plan includes a number of agents. If your team outgrows it, move up a plan or talk to us about Enterprise.

Does the Android app cost extra?

No. The app is included on every plan for every agent.

Can I use my existing WhatsApp or telephony provider?

Yes. DialSathi connects to Interakt, AiSensy, Wati and Gupshup for WhatsApp, and to Exotel, MCUBE and Knowlarity for calling. You keep your own account, and message and call charges are billed by that provider.

What happens when I hit a daily limit?

Daily limits reset every day. If your team regularly needs more, move up a plan or ask us about Enterprise limits.

How are the add-on modules priced?

HRMS, Sales & Billing, Recruitment and the AI Suite are priced for your company size. Book a demo and we'll send a quote.

Do I get a GST invoice?

Yes. Every payment through Razorpay comes with a GST invoice in your company's name.

Security & access

Your leads are yours. Salaries stay private.

A CRM holds your customers' phone numbers and, with HRMS, your team's pay. DialSathi keeps each company separate and lets each person see only what their job needs.

Audit log
Role checks

A separate database schema per company

Each company's data lives in its own PostgreSQL schema on its own subdomain. One customer's query can't reach another's rows.

Two checks on every action

Has your company switched this module on? Is this person allowed to do this? Both must say yes, on every request.

Audit trail

Important changes are written to an audit log, and recruitment keeps an append-only history on every application.

Roles

The right access for every job.

HR and accounts teams get full control of their own areas without becoming CRM admins. A manager approves leave and expenses but never sees a colleague's salary. A senior agent can fill an interview scorecard without seeing salary bands or offers.

AdminRuns the workspace: settings, agents, plans and every module.
ManagerLeads a team, distributes leads, approves leave and expenses.
Senior agentCalls and closes, and can take part in interviews.
AgentCalls, follows up and updates their own leads.
HRRuns HRMS and Recruitment, including payroll, with no access to leads.
AccountsRuns Sales & Billing and issues invoices, with no CRM admin rights.

TRAI DND checks

Bulk SMS and campaigns skip numbers on the Do Not Disturb registry before sending, and every message's delivery status is recorded.

Signed webhooks

Leads from IndiaMART, Meta and Google are only accepted when the webhook signature checks out. Generic webhooks use a private token per source.

About

Sathi means companion.

We named it DialSathi because that's the job: stay beside the telecaller all day, remember every number, every promise and every follow-up, so they can focus on the conversation.

Why we built it

Indian sales teams deserve software made for them.

Most CRMs are built for email-first sales in other countries. Indian teams sell on the phone and on WhatsApp, buy leads from IndiaMART and Meta, bill in GST and pay incentives every month. They end up stitching five tools together.

DialSathi puts that whole day in one place, from the first call to the invoice to the payslip, priced so a five-person team can start today.
